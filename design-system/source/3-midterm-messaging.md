# Midterm messaging · Nov 2026

A campaign layer on top of the voice for the final stretch before the Nov 3, 2026 midterms. Same voice (action over anxiety, in on the joke), aimed at four targets. The live set is the **MidtermSet** component. These lines are dated: retire or rewrite them after Nov 3.

## The four lanes

Every piece picks one lane, lands one joke, and ends with one action.

### 1. The cost of everything
The everyday bill: gas, groceries, rent, child care, tariffs. The joke is the gap between "prices are coming down" and the receipt.

- Gas is $4.20. Not a joke. *(Neither is Tuesday.)*
- Read the receipt. Then read the ballot.
- They called affordability a con job. Your receipt calls it Tuesday.
- "The party of affordability." Have you met a receipt?
- Your cart got smaller. Your ballot didn't.
- Fill the tank. Then fill in the bubble.
- Tax cuts at the top. Price hikes at the pump.

### 2. Check, please (the gold-plated White House)
The gold decor, the ballroom, the paved Rose Garden, and the Cheesecake Factory comparison that took over the week. The joke is the menu: lavish for them, nothing on it for you. Close the tab is the action.

- Check, please. *(Close the tab · Vote Nov 3)*
- Less Cheesecake Factory. More factories.
- Donors got a ballroom. You got the bill.
- Gold everything. Nothing on the menu for you.
- Affordability: 86'd.
- Your table's ready. Polls close at the end of the night.

### 3. For sale (the grift)
Self-dealing, donor favors, family business in the people's house. The joke is the swamp that got remodeled instead of drained.

- Drain the swamp? They put in a hot tub.
- Everything's for sale but your vote.
- Not a cabinet. A gift shop.
- The swamp got a renovation. You got the invoice.

### 4. Make it a short term (turnout)
The close. Every piece in the other three lanes can end here.

- Midterms Tuesday. Make it a short term.
- Four days. Every vote.
- Costume Saturday. Ballot Tuesday.
- The polls close. We don't.
- Democracy is a group project. Your part is due Nov 3.

## Rules for this round

- **Facts first, then the joke.** Every number is sourced and dated in the piece's notes: the $4.20 gas figure is the AAA national average for Oct 30, 2026. When a number moves, the line moves with it.
- **Quote only what was said.** "The party of affordability" and "con job" are the president's own words about affordability. Attribute or set them as quotes; never invent a quote or put words in a named person's mouth.
- **Punch at conduct, not people.** Mock the gold, the ballroom, the receipts, the self-dealing. No name-calling, no jokes about anyone's body, family or voters.
- **Grift, not crimes.** Say grift, self-dealing, for sale, donor's choice. Don't call anything a crime unless a charge or conviction says so.
- **The Cheesecake Factory is the punchline, not the target.** The chain and its diners are fine. The joke is the decor in the White House. No logos, no menu art, no trade dress.
- **Every piece ends with an action**: Vote Nov 3, make a plan, bring three friends, find a shift. Polling hours and rules vary by state; never print a single nationwide opening or closing time.
- Grounds follow the system: navy for cost of living, yellow for Check, please and turnout, red for who pays, chrome for grift, paper for receipts and other borrowed formats.
