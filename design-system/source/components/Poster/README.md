# Poster

Print and story-format poster, 1200×1800 (2:3), shown here at 25%.

**Provide:** a two-beat headline (six words or fewer per beat), one sentence of body that names a specific action, one pill CTA naming the program, and the unofficial mark.

Anatomy, top to bottom: wordmark stand-in (`wm`, 34px) → headline (`display-poster`, 196–300px, leading .86) → optional device (checklist, lock box, scene) → `sub` (40px Public Sans 600, max 20em) → footer row with pill CTA left and unofficial mark right, padding 90px.

Four grounds, one per piece:

| Ground | Headline | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy `c-navy-900` | paper text, yellow for the turn | The default. Halo pattern top-right. |
| Yellow `c-yellow-500` | navy | Upbeat asks; ink pill or none. |
| Sky gradient | cream, then butter | Climate and outdoors only; the one non-Con use of sky, kept to the upper half, with navy ground at the foot for body copy. |
| Paper `c-slate-50` | navy in a white lock box | Policy claims; red tag for the hook. |

- The strike-and-swap device (struck word in `c-slate-300` at 55% with a rotated `c-red-500` bar, then the replacement in yellow) is the system's signature move. One per piece.
- Yellow is the turn of the sentence, not decoration. Highlight one beat.
- Grounds are fixed brand colors; posters don't follow the viewer's theme.
