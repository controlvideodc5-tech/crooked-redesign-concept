# MerchLine

How a slogan becomes product: garment color, ink, and one line. The preview shows the actual mockups from the merch PDFs from Merch Round 2; see also **MerchLookbook** and **HatSeries**.

**Provide:** the line (from the voice section), the garment and its color, the print method and ink colors, and a placement. Mockup cards caption as **PRODUCT NAME** in display caps over a mono spec line ("Navy twill · yellow embroidery").

- Inks come from the brand palette: yellow, navy, white, cream, red 700, red 500. Garment colors may go beyond it: black `#0E1015`, forest `#1F3B2A`, natural canvas `#E8E0CC`, duck tan `#B98A4E`. Those are product colors, not tokens; never use them on screen.
- One idea per product. Series share one idea across items ("Everybody In": in, floor, eats, working people, all of us, build it here).
- Borrowed formats carry the best pieces: the record sleeve (Planet B-side), the tracklist (Greatest Hits), the jersey number (Voter 26), the national-park badge, the certificate mug.
- Mockups are flat garment silhouettes on paper (`c-slate-100`) with the unofficial mark in the corner. No models, no photo comps of real people.
