# Voice and tone

Talk like the smartest friend in the group chat. Crooked is for people who follow politics closely, are tired of being scared by it, and want somewhere to put the energy. The voice treats them as smart, busy and already on the team. It explains, it jokes, and it always ends with something to do.

## Four pillars

**Action over anxiety.** Name the problem in one beat, then turn to the move. Every piece leaves a door open: listen, show up, or organize.
> "The news, minus the panic." · "Down, not out." · "Doomscroll less. Doorknock more."

**In on the joke.** Self-aware and a little irreverent, especially about ourselves. The humor punches at the situation, never at the listener.
> "…and a few lowly podcast hosts." · "Hot takes. Yes. Twice a day, five days a week." · "Freedom & Content for All."

**Plainspoken.** Short words, real verbs, no insider fog. If a sentence needs a second read, it needs a rewrite. Concrete beats clever.
> "One email each weekday evening with what happened and what you can do about it."

**Independent and proud of it.** Membership keeps the network independent; it is never a donation plea. Confident, never pleading.
> "Your membership keeps Crooked independent and out of Big Tech's pocket." · "Ad-free. Fear-free."

## Flex by surface

| Surface | Dial toward | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero and section heads | Punchy, two beats, display caps | "Late-night politics. Live in LA." |
| Membership (Friends of the Pod) | Warm, value first, independence second | "Your favorite pods. Ad-free." |
| Organizing (Vote Save America) | Calm urgency, specific next step | "Find a shift, a donation match or a phone bank that fits your week." |
| Events (Crooked Con) | Most playful, spectacle, big stakes | "Freedom & Content for All" |
| Posters and socials | Two beats with a turn; the second beat is the action | "Ballot first. Brunch after." · "The polls close. We don't." |
| Stickers and merch | One line, wearable, gift-able, still true out of context | "Phone bank > fear bank" · "Hope is a verb" |
| Newsletter headers | A count plus a promise | "15 days. Every vote." |
| Product UI and microcopy | Quiet, literal, two or three words | "Up next" · "Saved" · "Hover a card to preview" |
| Commerce (Store) | Lightest touch | "A tee for the next protest or a gift for the Hysteria fan in your life." |

## Writing rules

**Headlines.** Two short beats split by a period, six words or fewer each. The second beat turns toward action. Write in sentence case; CSS sets the caps.

**Buttons and links.** Start with a verb: Play, Join, Find, Get, See, Shop. Say what happens: "Play today's lineup", not "Learn more". A pill CTA in collateral pairs the action with the program: "Find a shift · Vote Save America".

**Body.** One idea per sentence, under 25 words. Ledes stop at three sentences. Contractions always. Second person always.

**Numbers and dates.** Metadata uses abbreviated months, an en dash for ranges and a middle dot as separator: `Nov 5–7 · Washington, DC`. Never `11/5/26`. Counts in headlines are numerals: "15 days."

**Errors and UI.** Literal. "That didn't load. Try again." Save the jokes for editorial.

## Rewrites

| Instead of | Write | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~~URGENT: Democracy is on the line. Donate now!~~ | Down, not out. Find a phone bank that fits your week. | Action over anxiety. A specific ask beats an alarm. |
| ~~Support our mission with a monthly contribution.~~ | Your favorite pods. Ad-free. | Lead with what the member gets. |
| ~~Click here to learn more about our podcasts.~~ | Find your show | Verb first, no filler. |
| ~~Stay informed with our daily newsletter.~~ | The news, minus the panic | Promise a feeling, not a category. |
| ~~Make sure you vote this November!~~ | Democracy is a group project. Your part is due Nov 3. | Borrow a familiar format and give a deadline. |
| ~~Oops! Something went wrong 😬~~ | That didn't load. Try again. | UI copy stays literal. |

## What the voice never does

- Fearmonger, all-caps-shout or count down to doom.
- Mock the listener, or anyone for being new to politics.
- Plead for money. Membership is value first.
- Put words in a real person's mouth. Quotes are real, attributed and checked.
- Use emoji in brand voice. Quoted chat voices may.
